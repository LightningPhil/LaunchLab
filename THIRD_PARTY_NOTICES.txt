Planet surface textures — Solar System Scope / INOVE
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

Sun, Mercury, Venus atmosphere, Earth day map, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn,
Uranus, Neptune and Moon. Source maps are based on NASA imagery/elevation
with artistic colour adjustments and some fictional gap filling.
LaunchLab resizes and converts the maps to WebP, then applies globe shading.
No endorsement by the creators is implied.

Pluto, Ganymede and Charon surface textures — NASA Visualization Technology
Applications and Development (VTAD), extracted from NASA's public 3D models:
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/pluto-3d-model/
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/ganymede-3d-model/
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/charon-3d-model/
NASA media usage guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
LaunchLab resizes and converts the base-colour maps to WebP and applies
globe shading. These are visualization assets; source detail varies by region.
NASA material is used for educational visualization, without endorsement.
Exact downloads, native dimensions and checksums: tools/world-texture-sources.json.
